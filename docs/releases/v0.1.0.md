First release of **Tropix**, an island and afro ROMpler for Akai MPC standalone devices (native ARM VST2, MPC OS 3.x).
Tested on an MPC Live II, MPC OS 3.9.1.

- **128 presets**, browsed by genre (afrobeat, amapiano, kompa, soca, reggae, Hawaiian) and type (keys, guitars,
  horns, steel pan, mallets, bass, percussion, synths & FX); also in MPC's own PRESET list
- **65 instruments** from redistributable recordings (CC0, CC BY 3.0, University of Iowa MIS) and original synthesis:
  steel pans, amapiano log drums, saxes and trumpets, kompa and highlife electrics, slack-key and palm-wine
  acoustics, ukulele, pedal and lap steel, pianos, e-pianos, organs, mallets, basses and percussion
- **Two layers** per preset with level, pan, octave, fine, on and solo
- **Horn playing:** glide, scoop, delayed vibrato, mono / legato modes; pad pressure adds vibrato
- Cutoff, resonance, bright, drive, envelope, LFO (tremolo, filter or auto-pan), chorus, tempo-synced delay, reverb
- Favourites, nine colorways (Sunset, Lagos, Aloha, Jamaica, Rasta, Haiti, Africa, Kanaval, Jozi Night), 16 Q-Links

Install: put `Tropix-0.1.0-mpc-armv7.zip` on the MPC and install it like any other plugin (INSTALL.md is inside the
zip). Sound credits: `engine/LICENSES.md` in the plugin folder.
