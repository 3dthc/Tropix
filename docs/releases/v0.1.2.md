**Tropix 0.1.2** for Akai MPC standalone devices (native ARM VST2).
Tested on an MPC Live II (first generation), MPC OS 3.10.

- **SETTINGS:** A4 tuning, transpose, bend range, polyphony, velocity and stereo spread are now full-size knobs:
  touch one and drag, or turn the data wheel. Voice mode, LFO target, horn scoop, velocity curve, mod wheel and
  wave animation stay as rows you tap to step through.
- **LAYERS:** level, pan, octave and fine change with the data wheel or by dragging; a layer's sound too.
- **Lighter on the CPU:** an idle Tropix costs almost nothing, and busy playing costs about a quarter less.
- Three downloads: `-mpc-armv7.zip` for first-generation MPCs on MPC OS 3 (MPC Live / Live II, One, X, Key 61, Force);
  `-mpc-aarch64.zip` for second-generation MPCs (untested there); `-mpc-os2.zip` for first-generation MPCs still on
  MPC OS 2.x (the same build as the armv7 zip, which loads and draws there; untested on 2.x)

Install: put the zip for your MPC and MPC OS on it and install it like any other plugin (INSTALL.md is inside the zip). Projects
saved with 0.1.0 or 0.1.1 open with their sounds and settings.
