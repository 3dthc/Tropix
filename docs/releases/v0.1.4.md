## Changes

- **Projects are saved as one block.** A project now keeps the preset, your edits and every setting (tuning, theme,
  layers, voices, effects) together, and reopening it puts all of it back in one go. The edited marker shows
  straight away instead of a moment later.
- **Favourites are kept for good.** Every Tropix on the MPC shares one favourites list: a star stays in every project and
  every new instance until you take it off, shows in every open Tropix at once, and survives upgrades (it's kept in the
  plugin folder's `user/` folder). Projects saved with favourites from 0.1.2 or earlier add them to the list.

## Fixes

- A new Tropix (or a new project) no longer starts with an empty ★ LIST list.
- Reopening a project no longer lands on a different preset. If the data wheel was the last thing used on the LCD
  (after touching a preset in the list, ◀ ▶ or a tab) before saving, the project used to open one preset further
  on, sometimes without your edits. Projects saved with earlier versions open on the right preset too.
- Projects saved with 0.1.3 or earlier still open with their sounds and settings.
