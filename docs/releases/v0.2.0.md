**Tropix 0.2.0**: arpeggiator, patterns and chords.

- **New ARP view** with its own button on the foot: ARP (up, down, up-down, as played, random; 1 to 4 octaves), PATTERN
  (16 genre phrases built on your lowest key, with chord hits that play every held key) and CHORD (one key, one chord,
  with a strum). Rates 1/4 to 1/32 with dotted and triplet values, gate, swing, latch.
- Steps sit on MPC's song position: in time across loops and tempo changes, running on their own at the tempo when stopped.
- The sustain pedal holds the arp's notes. The arp settings stay when you change presets.
- Changed: the favourites list is now the **★ FAVS** row under the genres (its foot button made room for ARP).
- Offline-tested (arp module and the real engine, under ASan); not yet played on a device.
